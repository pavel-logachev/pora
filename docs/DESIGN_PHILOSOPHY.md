# Measured Care

Measured Care treats a health-adjacent interface as a piece of calm infrastructure. The indigo field establishes reliability without borrowing clinical-green clichés; warm paper surfaces keep the product human, while a small coral signal marks the moment that requires action. The visual system does not imply diagnosis or medical authority.

The composition follows the product loop: plan, remind, record. Device screenshots remain real and legible instead of being replaced by fictional mockups. Wide editorial margins separate evidence from claims, and the phone frames are deliberately quiet so the application's actual hierarchy remains primary.

Typography is direct and operational. Large labels identify the product; small tracked metadata communicates platform and status. Decorative elements are limited to schedule lines, checkpoints and the existing application icon, making the public GitHub presentation feel like the same product rather than a marketing skin.
